## Microsoft Ontology Playground

This version's graph presentation and interaction design is informed by
[Microsoft Ontology Playground](https://github.com/microsoft/Ontology-Playground),
including its fCoSE layout approach, circular entity treatment, curved edge
routing, focus-context highlighting and floating canvas controls.

The current release retains the layout, routing and focus-context ideas while
restoring the product's earlier rounded-rectangle entity presentation.
